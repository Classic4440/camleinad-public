Who Is CAM?

Cam Leinad is the reverse of Daniel Mac — literally and musically. The artist behind the name is Muwonge Daniel Mark, born in Uganda on 16 May 2004 and later embracing life across multiple countries including the United Kingdom and South Korea.

The Story

His debut single, "How It Starts," captures the quiet, electric moment just before everything changes — a shimmering mix of modern vulnerability and timeless pop craft.

The name Cam Leinad is "Daniel Mac" reversed. CAM is the short form — and also the record label: Cam All May. Three names, one identity: born Daniel Mac, becoming Cam Leinad, releasing under Cam All May.

The Music

Heartfelt pop melodies with the soothing warmth of easy listening. Open-hearted anthems for late-night thoughts and fresh starts.

The Label

CAM stands for Cam All May — the independent record label founded by Cam Leinad. It's also the artist's nickname, shortened from Cam Leinad. Everything you hear under CAM is released independently, without a major label, on the artist's own terms.
